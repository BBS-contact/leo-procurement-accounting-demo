Procurement & Accounting Anomaly Review Dashboard

LEO reviews procurement and accounting records, detects unusual patterns, explains why they may matter, and prepares evidence for a human reviewer. It does not decide guilt, block payments, mutate records, or replace institutional judgment.

What LEO analyzes Prices, quantities, supplier capacity, declared business activity, threshold-near fragments, and evidence consistency.
What LEO shows Risk signals, affected suppliers, supporting evidence IDs, reason text, review priority, and a human review memo.
What humans decide Whether a signal is justified, should be escalated, is a false positive, or requires more documents.
Institutional value LEO helps an organization notice weak procurement records before they become audit, governance, donor, or legal problems.
Practical use A reviewer can prioritize work, request missing documents, and preserve a transparent evidence trail.
Control model The system supports human judgment. It does not replace management, accounting, legal review, or formal audit authority.

What LEO does not do

  • 0No autonomous enforcement, blocking, payment decision, supplier punishment, or legal conclusion.
  • 0No canonical registry, production file, key, signature, or external system is opened or mutated.
  • 0Human review choices are local browser notes unless explicitly exported by the reviewer.
  • 0The dashboard is an evidence review layer, not an operational authority.

How to read this demo

A first-time reviewer can follow this sequence without knowing the internal LEO architecture.
Step 1
Read the summary
Start with findings, suppliers, high-risk count, and autonomous actions.
Step 2
Check what LEO found
Look at signal families: price, supplier capacity, activity mismatch, quantity, and fragmentation.
Step 3
Review priorities
Open the strongest suppliers and findings before reviewing medium-risk items.
Step 4
Inspect evidence
Use evidence IDs to trace each signal back to the demo report.
Step 5
Record human judgment
Mark each finding as justified, escalated, false positive, or needing more evidence.
Step 6
Export memo
Export a local human review package without changing production or canonical records.
